TOOLS OF THE TRADE

Skills.

Explore the tools I work with and the things I can create with them.

01 HTML ★★★★★ +

Building the structure of websites using HTML. Creating organised content, page layouts, links and accessible elements.

02 CSS ★★★★★ +

Styling websites with colours, typography, layouts, spacing and responsive design. Bringing the visual side of a website to life.

03 JavaScript ★★★★★ +

Learning the fundamentals of JavaScript, including variables, functions, logic and interactive website elements.

04 Adobe Photoshop ★★★★★ +

Editing and manipulating images, working with layers, making adjustments and creating visual compositions.

05 Adobe Illustrator ★★★★★ +

Creating vector illustrations, logos, visual identities, icons and graphics for both digital and printed applications.

06 Adobe InDesign ★★★★★ +

Designing print layouts such as brochures, menus, flyers and other multi-page documents. Preparing designs for print.

07 Figma ★★★★★ +

Creating interface designs, wireframes, prototypes and visual layouts for digital products and websites.

08 SketchUp ★★★★★ +

Creating basic 3D models and visualisations to explore shapes, objects and spatial designs.

09 Signage ★★★★★ +

Designing graphics for physical spaces, including advertising boards, banners, stickers and other large-format printed applications.

10 Creative thinking ★★★★★ +

Developing original ideas, exploring different approaches to design and finding creative solutions to visual problems.
